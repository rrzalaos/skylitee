Section 3 — Revenue vs Spend Chart
Unique value: Every tool shows revenue OR spend. We show both on the same timeline so you see immediately if spend went up but revenue didn't move — the signal that something broke.
Add a third layer nobody shows: COD cancellation dips. A revenue spike that's followed by a dip 3 days later = COD returns. We can show gross revenue vs net-of-returns on the same chart.

Section 6 — Conversion Funnel
Unique value: No single tool shows the full D2C funnel in one view. Meta shows top-of-funnel. GA4 shows mid-funnel. Shopify shows bottom. We stitch all three.
The unique insight: where the biggest drop-off is and why.

"You lose 84% of people between Click and Add to Cart. This is a landing page problem, not a targeting problem. Your CTR is fine."

Nobody says that. Every other tool just shows the numbers and leaves you to figure out the diagnosis.

Section 7 — Alerts & Signals
Unique value: Every platform sends its own alerts in isolation. Meta alerts about ad delivery. Shopify alerts about inventory. GSC sends email reports. Nobody connects them.
Unique alerts we surface that nobody else does:

"Meta spend up 40% this week but Shopify orders flat — possible attribution break or landing page issue" (cross-platform)
"COD% jumped from 52% to 71% in 3 days — check if new campaign is targeting high-COD geographies" (Shopify + Meta combined)
"GSC position dropped 4 spots on your top keyword — check if you changed that page recently" (requires GSC history + context)
"Lead campaign frequency at 3.8 — your CPL will start rising in 2–3 days" (predictive, not reactive)
"Repeat rate 3% vs 20% industry benchmark — 31 customers who could come back haven't been emailed" (benchmark + action)
